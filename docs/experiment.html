The Sound Shapes Experiment

Some sounds seem to have shapes. What happens when you trust your first instinct?

PART 1 OF 3

Look at these two shapes. Which one should be called KIKI?

Trust your instinct. There’s no personal grade here.✳

What is going on?

In sound-symbolism experiments, many people connect sharper-sounding words with angular shapes and softer-sounding words with round shapes. It isn’t a universal rule, and it certainly isn’t a test of intelligence.

The point is to notice how language can engage feeling, association and perception—not just conscious definitions. This experience is inspired by research on the “bouba/kiki” effect, rather than a formal Suggestopedic exercise by Lonny.
