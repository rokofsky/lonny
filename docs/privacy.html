What is collected?

When you send an enquiry or a question, the site stores your name (if provided), email and message. Event registration requests store your contact details and selected session. Community word submissions store only the display name and text you choose.

What happens next?

The site keeps incoming messages in a private administrator dashboard so Lonny can read and respond. He decides whether to publish selected questions and approved community responses; email addresses are never displayed on public pages. There are no automatic marketing emails.
