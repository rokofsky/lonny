Lonny Gold is a Canadian educator and teacher trainer who has worked with Suggestopedia since 1978. He leads language-learning, teacher-development and communication experiences internationally, bringing imagination, meaningful interaction and psychological safety into the learning environment. THE THINKING BEHIND IT

What is Suggestopedia?

Suggestopedia is an approach to teaching and learning associated with Georgi Lozanov. Its practice pays attention to the learning environment, relationships, emotion, music, imagination and the ways people respond beyond conscious effort.

Lonny’s work uses these ideas as a foundation for vivid language and teacher-training experiences. The easiest way to understand his approach may be to try an activity for yourself.
